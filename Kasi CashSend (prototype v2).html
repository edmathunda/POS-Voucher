Kasi CashSend

Concept prototype, version 2. The sender pays all fees and the recipient pays nothing. The tuckshop owner earns a commission, and cash is only settled once both sides confirm. Follow the money from sender to recipient to shop owner. Fees and limits are placeholders.

Behind the screen

Checks and controls, updated as you go.
    Placeholder rules
    FeesSender pays the Send Cash fee plus a R6.00 tuckshop fee. Recipient pays R0.00.
    Owner commissionR3.00 plus 1% of the cash. Paid on settlement. Held 24 hours if the customer did not confirm. Taken back if a dispute is upheld against the shop.
    VoucherR100 to R500, one full cash-out, locked to the recipient's number, valid 30 days.
    LimitsR1,000 per recipient per day. Shop cash float capped at R3,000.
    Cash code6 digits, single use, 10 minutes, one shop only.
    Two-sided confirmShop confirms handover, customer confirms receipt. If the customer is silent for 2 hours the cash settles and the commission waits 24 hours.
    RefundsSender is refunded all fees if cancelled before a code is issued. Unclaimed vouchers return to the sender and the tuckshop fee is refunded.
