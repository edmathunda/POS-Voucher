Critical System Architecture Overlays

Offline Resilience & Edge-Case Fallback

To operate reliably within informal retail sectors prone to load-shedding and infrastructure constraints, the terminal layer houses a local validation overlay. Encrypted cryptographic handshakes are securely queued locally inside the container when device communication dropouts occur, safeguarding trade continuity without risking double-spend vulnerability.

Decentralized Liquidity Management

The platform prevents structural cash depletion at the local tuckshop tier through real-time algorithmic tracking. Real-time token monitoring and webhook callbacks allow field teams to orchestrate localized cash logistics and balance retail floats safely without creating transaction bottlenecks.
